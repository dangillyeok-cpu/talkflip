How to Play & Guides

No install — just a link. Set the players and the occasion, and the right modes and decks are ready; check how to play, then flip cards one at a time.

Basic flow

  • ① Players — set how many of you are playing (solo, two, or a group).
  • ② Occasion — pick First meeting, Friends, Couple, Drinks, Family, or Party, and only the modes and decks that fit light up.
  • ③ Mode & deck — auto-suggested for your occasion. Adjust right there if you like.
  • ④ Start → see how to play (the rule) → flip cards one at a time!

Occasion presets

New to "modes and decks"? No problem. Just pick an occasion and the right play style and cards are set up for you. For example, Couple starts with This-or-That + the Couple deck, and Drinks with Who's Most Likely + the Party deck. Tap Start and you'll see the chosen mode's how-to-play once before the cards begin.

Modes

Who's Most Likely

Read “Who here is most likely to…?” and everyone points to one person. The most-voted person gives a short defense for extra fun. 3+ players.

This or That

Pick A or B and everyone says it out loud at once! The minority side explains first for extra fun. 1+ players.

Classic Flip

Take turns answering questions. Great for first meetings and casual settings. 1+ players.

Hot Seat

One person sits in the “hot seat.” Draw 3 cards, answer 2, then pass to the next person. 2+ players.

Taboo Round

One person describes a topic without saying the banned words. Everyone else catches slips. 3+ players.

Cards you love

Save them with the heart and share them with friends. You can also share a recap when a round ends. Switch the language (Korean / English) anytime in Settings.
