  2. Who here gives the strongest 'I have a story' face?
  3. Who here would become obsessed with a random hobby overnight?
  4. Who here would name the group chat something weird?
  5. Who here would order one thing and regret not ordering another?
  6. Who here would turn a simple plan into a full spreadsheet?
  7. Who here would laugh first in a serious moment?
  8. Who here would accidentally start a group debate?
  9. Who'd nail a surprise talent show?
  10. Who laughs at their own jokes the most?
  11. Who writes delivery reviews like a novel?
  12. Who greets the dog before the owner?
  13. Who gets stuck longest at self-checkout?
  14. Who only sings ballads at karaoke?
  15. Who takes 200 photos on one trip?
  16. Who mashes the elevator close button the most?
  17. Who gets caught slacking in a meeting?

This or that 13 cards · Pick one at the same time

  1. Main character energy or peaceful background character?
  2. Perfect timing or perfect charm?
  3. Never lose your keys or never lose your charger?
  4. Be funny in texts or funny in person?
  5. Always know the gossip or always avoid the drama?
  6. Win every small argument or never get embarrassed?
  7. Be slightly late forever or slightly overdressed forever?
  8. Have elite snack taste or elite meme taste?
  9. Accidentally send the wrong text or accidentally like an old post?
  10. Have too much confidence or too much curiosity?
  11. Funny but plain, or gorgeous but boring?
  12. Only one meme forever or no memes ever?
  13. Sneeze glitter or burp bubbles?
