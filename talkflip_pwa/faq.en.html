Frequently Asked Questions

The things people ask most about TalkFlip.

Is TalkFlip free?

Yes, completely free. No install or sign-up — just open a link and play.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs right in your web browser. If you play often, add it to your home screen to enjoy it full-screen like an app.

How many players do I need?

It depends on the mode. Classic and This or That work from 1 player, Hot Seat needs 2+, and Who's Most Likely and Taboo shine with 3+. Set your player count and only the matching modes and decks light up.

Does it work offline?

After your first visit, cards work offline in most cases. Adding it to your home screen makes it even more reliable.

Do you store my data?

Favorites and play stats are stored only on your device (browser storage) and never sent to a server. You can reset them anytime in Settings. See our Privacy Policy for details.

When is it best to use?

First meetings, friend hangouts, couples, work dinners, family, parties. Pick an occasion on the first screen and the right play style and cards are set up for you.

How do I add it to my home screen?

On iPhone (Safari): Share button → 'Add to Home Screen'. On Android (Chrome): Menu (⋮) → 'Add to Home screen'.

Can I suggest question cards?

Yes! Send your questions on the Idea Board and good cards get added to the game after review.
