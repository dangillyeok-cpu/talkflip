Penalty Wheel

Stuck on "so what's the penalty?" One tap and the wheel decides.

The most-voted person on a point card, the smaller side on a this-or-that, whoever passes on a question. When you need a penalty but can't be bothered to invent one, spin. Most penalties work without alcohol, so non-drinkers and café tables are fine. One phone is all it takes.

▶ Spin the wheel

Opens inside the TalkFlip game. The Penalty button on point cards is the same wheel.

How to use it

  • Point cards: most-voted person → Penalty button → spin.
  • This or that: the smaller side takes a penalty. Tie means everyone.
  • Pass on a question, take a penalty. The Spicy deck runs on this rule.
  • Keep it decent. Swap any penalty you hate for "tell one embarrassing story".
